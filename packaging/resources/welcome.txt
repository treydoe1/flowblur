FlowBlur adds an optical-flow motion blur effect to Adobe Premiere Pro (and After Effects).

This installer copies FlowBlur.plugin to:
/Library/Application Support/Adobe/Common/Plug-ins/7.0/MediaCore/

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon. Quit Premiere Pro and After Effects before continuing; they load the plug-in when they start.

Any previously installed FlowBlur.plugin at that location is replaced.
